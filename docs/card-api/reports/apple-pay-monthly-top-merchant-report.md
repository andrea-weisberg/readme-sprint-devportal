<p data-renderer-start-pos="10987">For clients using Paymentology’s tokenization, Paymentology can issue monthly reports to clients to assist in compiling their Apple report through the Apple Partner Connect platform.</p>
<p data-renderer-start-pos="10987">The purpose of this report is for Apple to understand the top 100 Merchants that accept Apple Pay. It’s important to note only approved/settled transactions are reported and Merchants are listed in descending order based on total spend.</p>
<p>The report includes the following details:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Rank </strong>– Merchant rank in descending order based on % of total Apple Pay transaction (spend).</li>
<li><strong>Top 100 Merchants</strong> – this is the merchant description.</li>
<li><strong>% of Total Apple Pay Transaction (Spend)</strong> – this is the merchants total spend amount as a percent of the overall Apple Pay transaction spend.</li>
<li><strong>Transaction Spend</strong> – this is the total value in Apple Pay transactions spent at the merchant within the given month.</li>
<li><strong>Transaction Count</strong> – this is the total count of Apple Pay transactions at the merchant within the given month.</li>
</ul>
