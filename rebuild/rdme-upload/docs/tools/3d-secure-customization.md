The 3D Secure Screen Customization tool is available to clients who have already signed up with Paymentology and are using the Sprint platform and are implementing the 3D Secure functionality for your chosen API (Card or Companion). We support different cardholder authentication methods to suit your needs, please choose the tool corresponding to the method you are implementing with Paymentology.
